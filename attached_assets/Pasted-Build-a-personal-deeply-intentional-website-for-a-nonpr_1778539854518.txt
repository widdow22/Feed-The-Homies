Build a personal, deeply intentional website for a nonprofit called Feed the Homies.
The feeling:
The vibe is late night, introspective, emotionally honest. Think someone who spent weeks perfecting their Tumblr or MySpace page — personal, warm, a little maximalist but everything means something. The music playing in the background of this page would be Prod Marvin, late night R&B, soft rap. It's 2am and someone who really cares made this. Not an agency. A person.
This is not a charity website. It is a portal. A place people feel found.
Visual direction:

Warm dark tones — deep burgundy, aged cream, soft black, dusty rose accents
Mix of a serif or handwritten-feeling font for emotional moments and a clean sans-serif for structure
Feels layered — like stickers, notes, and intention all in one place
Subtle textures okay — aged paper, grain — nothing too heavy
Spotify embed for the Feed the Homies playlist: https://open.spotify.com/playlist/6jlUN17DYWF20m6oX3Z1Kx
Small personal touches everywhere — like someone left little notes for you to find

Sections:

Opening — full screen, minimal. Just the name and one line that feels like a letter opener. Something like "you found us." or "this is for you." Cursor or subtle animation okay.
About — written like a personal note, not a mission statement. First person. Raw. "This started because..." Handwritten-style font for pull quotes.
The Playlist — embed the Spotify playlist. Frame it like "this is what we're made of" or "put this on first."
What we do — Feed. Wear. Remember. But presented like journal entries or pinned notes, not a corporate grid.
Hoodie drop — feels like a secret you're letting them in on. Limited. Rare. Quiet. Not a store page.
Join us — email signup that feels like leaving your number with a friend, not subscribing to a newsletter.
Footer — something small and human. A quote, a city, a feeling.

Technical:

Fully responsive
Spotify embed must work
Subtle scroll animations if possible — nothing flashy, just things fading or sliding in softly
Email signup confirms with something warm, not "thank you for subscribing"
No stock photos. Type, texture, and color do everything.

Most important: Make it feel like a person poured themselves into this. Every corner should feel intentional and personal. If it starts looking like a template, start over.
